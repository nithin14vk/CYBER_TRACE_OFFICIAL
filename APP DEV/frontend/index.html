YOUR DIGITAL FOOTPRINT IS MORE VISIBLE THAN YOU THINK.

While modern browser sandboxing prevents websites from stealing your local files or reading SIM cards, the benign telemetry needed for web rendering is frequently weaponized for cross-site tracking and spear-phishing.

Defensive Hardening Checklist

Protect yourself against device fingerprinting, credential stuffing, and phishing traps.

✔

Verify URLs & Domains

Always inspect the full domain name and TLS certificate before entering any credentials or corporate logins.

✔

Never Share OTPs or Recovery Codes

Authentication codes sent via SMS or authenticator apps should never be read aloud or entered on unverified URLs.

✔

Deploy FIDO2 Hardware MFA

Hardware security keys (YubiKey) are cryptographically bound to the legitimate domain, neutralizing reverse-proxy phishing.

✔

Audit Browser Permissions Regularly

Set Location, Camera, and Microphone to "Always Block" or "Ask Every Time", and revoke stale site permissions.

✔

Resist Device Fingerprinting

Use privacy browsers (Brave, Firefox with privacy.resistFingerprinting, Tor) that mask WebGL and clamp CPU core counts.

✔

Inspect Shortened Links

Use link expansion tools or curl to verify where shortened URLs redirect before opening them.
